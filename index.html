WP 01 / Origin

Left out of his first competition. On the podium fourteen times since.

Rokith's path into robotics began with a setback. When his school hosted its first robotics competition, he was left off the team for lacking experience, and his ability was openly questioned.

He chose to answer with results. Since then, he has dedicated himself to robotics and unmanned aerial systems, learning by building and testing his own hardware.

Over three years, he has built hands-on experience in freestyle, racing, line-of-sight and mapping operations, flying aircraft that he builds and tunes himself. All of his piloting experience has been gained on real hardware, without simulator training.

His portfolio now includes racing drones, competition robots and Agrifly, an agricultural drone system that won a silver medal at an international robotics championship and secured a US$5,000 investment.

    WP 03 / Projects

    Drones that do a job.

    Precision agriculture · Solo project

    Agrifly

    Silver Robowunder International Robotics Championship, Malaysia, 2026

    Agrifly is an AI-powered, IoT-integrated agricultural drone system designed for precision farming and smart crop monitoring.

    It uses multispectral imaging, NDVI analysis and environmental sensors to assess crop health and detect plant stress. By combining AI with real-time data analytics, Agrifly helps farmers improve productivity, optimise resources and farm more sustainably.

    Hardware & stack

    • 10-inch spraying & monitoring airframe
    • ESP32
    • MQ-135 gas sensor
    • MPU6050 IMU
    • BME280 temp / humidity / pressure
    • BH1750 light sensor
    • Soil-moisture sensor
    • NEO-6M GPS
    • Multispectral imaging
    • NDVI analysis
    • IoT cloud telemetry
    • Live web dashboard

    CleanSight

    An autonomous waste-detection system that works from a drone's view, made for the Greater Chennai Corporation.

    The drone surveys an area from the air and the system picks out waste in the footage on its own, so that garbage build-up can be found from above instead of by street-by-street inspection.
